Is UKAY really secondhand only?

Yes — every listing is pre-owned, vintage, or deadstock. No fast-fashion dropshipping. We verify sellers and remove fakes fast.

How do bidding and live auctions work?

Timed listings accept bids until the clock hits zero. Live auctions happen Fridays 8PM — sellers present pieces on video, you tap to bid, winners check out in-app.

What if my item isn't as described?

Every order is covered: photo evidence → instant refund or return label. Sellers with repeat offenses are banned.

How much does it cost to sell?

Listing is free. 6% fee only when you sell — and your first 5 sales are 0% fee during launch.
